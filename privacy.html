Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

This policy explains what the Lilu app for Android (“Lilu”) does with information. Lilu is made by McNolgie (Sarah Ortiz) (“we”, “us”). Questions: mcnolgie@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Lilu works without an account. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use your advertising ID.
  • Your music, your library and your listening history stay on your device. We don't receive them.
  • A few features you choose to use connect to the internet or to other devices. Each one is listed below with exactly what leaves your device and who receives it.
  • We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

1. What stays on your device

Lilu keeps the following in its private storage on your phone. We can't see it, and it is deleted when you uninstall Lilu or clear its storage:

  • Your library: the folders you add, and what Lilu reads from your files (title, artist, album, artwork, duration, lyrics and similar tags).
  • Your activity: playlists, likes, play counts, listening history, audiobook progress and bookmarks, recent searches, saved web streams, equalizer and playback settings, themes and images you choose.
  • Purchases: a record of what you bought through Google Play, so you can use it.

Files Lilu saves to your folders when you ask (edited tags, lyrics and cover art, trimmed copies, ringtones, exports) are your files: they stay on your phone after you uninstall Lilu until you delete them. Exports you make with Migration Tools are saved only where you choose; they contain no audio, file locations or account details, and Lilu never sends them anywhere.

3. Features that connect to other devices or the internet

Voice search

When you tap the microphone, your phone's speech recognition turns your words into text. Lilu tries on-device recognition first. If your phone doesn't offer it, or it can't make out your words, Lilu uses your phone's built-in speech service (often provided by Google) and asks it to work offline when it can; depending on your phone, that service may process the audio on its provider's servers under the provider's privacy terms. Lilu receives only the text. Searches from the Search screen are kept in your recent searches, which you can clear.

Listen Together

Listen Together connects phones near each other so everyone hears the same music. Phones find each other with Bluetooth and connect directly over the same Wi-Fi network; no server of ours is involved. The host's phone sends the audio and now-playing details (title, artist, album, artwork, lyrics and the upcoming queue) directly to the phones that join. If anyone sets a name to show to others, that name is shared with everyone in the session. This connection is encrypted. Auto-Sync briefly listens through a guest's microphone to measure delay; the sound is analysed on that phone and never saved or sent.

Casting to speakers and TVs

If you cast, Lilu uses Google Cast to find your device. For songs on your phone, Lilu runs a temporary server on your home network, at a private random address that stops when casting ends, so the speaker or TV can play the song. The Cast device receives the song and its details (title, artist, album and where the file is stored on your phone). For a web stream, the Cast device connects to the stream's address on the internet itself. Google's terms apply to Google Cast.

Web streams

If you play a web stream (Play Web Link) or a saved web stream, Lilu connects to the address you entered. The stream's operator receives what any internet request carries, such as your IP address and the app name and version, and its own privacy terms apply. Some streams use unencrypted connections.

Find Lyrics Online and sharing

“Find Lyrics Online” opens a Google search in your browser for the song's title and artist. When you share something, Android's share sheet sends it to the app you pick: a song file, a web stream's address, or, for Store songs, a link to our website with a short preview.

Links Lilu makes for Store songs and Listen Together invitations point to our website. Opening one in a browser reaches our website host like any web page (with your IP address); the private part of an invitation isn't sent to the website.

Your phone's media controls

Like other music apps, Lilu shows what's playing (title, artist, artwork) on your lock screen, in notifications and on connected Bluetooth devices. If you use Android Auto, Wear OS or Google Assistant, they can browse your Lilu library to play from it.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you connect Google Drive in Cloud Backup, Lilu saves a copy of your Lilu data in the hidden app-data area of your own Google Drive. It includes your library list (with file locations and tags), playlists, likes, play counts and history, audiobook progress, saved web streams, settings, equalizer presets, the images you added or created in Lilu (including a smaller copy of photos you picked for custom themes) and your purchase record (including Google Play order numbers, purchase tokens and Google's signed purchase details, so purchases can be restored). Your audio files are not uploaded. We don't run a server for this and can't read it; Google stores it under your Google account's terms. Lilu keeps the connected account's email address on your phone to show which account is connected and to remove Lilu's access when you disconnect. Auto-Sync turns on when you connect, and you can turn it off.

Disconnecting stops backups and removes Lilu's access to your Drive, but doesn't delete the copy. To delete it, open Google Drive on the web (drive.google.com), then Settings → Manage apps → Lilu → Options → Delete hidden app data.

Purchases and tips

Purchases and Coffee Break tips are processed by Google Play. We never receive your card or payment details. Google Play gives Lilu the purchase details (product, order number, purchase token, time and status), which Lilu checks on your device. With some purchases Lilu also sends Google Play a random ID that ties the purchase to the choice you made (for example the songs you picked); it isn't linked to you.

Store downloads (when the Store is available)

Lilu downloads the Store's catalog, previews and items from our content host. Like any download, these requests include your IP address and standard technical details. Lilu checks the catalog when it starts and when you return to it (at most once a minute), even if you don't open the Store, and, while Store Updates is on, once a day in the background; you can turn Store Updates off in Settings.

To download something you bought, Lilu sends our server the purchase details (product, order number and purchase token). The server confirms them with Google Play and keeps a record (product, order number, items and date) so you can download your purchases again and so one purchase can't be misused. We keep these records while we offer the Store. To limit abuse, the server also keeps short-lived counters tied to a hashed form of your IP address and of the purchase token; they are deleted automatically, usually within a day. Requests to our server include a Google check (Firebase App Check with Play Integrity) that confirms they come from a genuine copy of Lilu. Our server and Google Cloud keep standard request logs, including IP addresses, for up to 30 days. Our server runs on Google Cloud (Firebase) in the United States.

Trying a shared song. If a friend shares a Store song with you and you open the link to try it, Lilu sends the song's ID to our server (with your IP address, as above) to start a 7-day trial. Lilu keeps the trial on your phone and reminds you before it ends.

Feedback (only when you send it)

In Feedback & Support you can send us a message in one of two ways. Nothing is sent until you press send.

  • Send to Lilu: your message (Lilu removes links, email addresses, file paths, phone numbers and similar details by default; you can choose to keep them), the feedback type, app version, Android version, phone model, a random report number, the time we received it and, only if you type one, a reply email address. It is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) for us.
  • Send with my email app: Lilu opens your own email app with the same details (without a report number) addressed to us; your email provider sends it.

We use feedback only to answer you and improve Lilu, and we keep it for 12 months.

Crash reports (only if you allow them)

Lilu asks once whether to send anonymous crash reports, and you can change your answer any time in Settings → About Lilu → Crash Reports. If you allow them, Google Firebase Crashlytics receives a report when Lilu crashes or hits an error: the technical error details, app version, phone model, Android version, the phone's state at the time (such as free memory) and a random installation ID created by Crashlytics. Lilu removes personal details such as file paths and links from the error messages it reports itself; reports of crashes inside Android code can still contain technical details such as a file name. Reports never include your audio files or your library list. Google keeps crash reports for 90 days.

4. Who receives information

Only the parties named above, and only for the feature you use: Google (Google Play, Google Cast, Google Drive, Firebase and, depending on your phone, speech recognition), the web stream operators you choose, the nearby phones, Cast devices, cars and watches you connect to, the apps you share to, and the companies that host our website and server. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to a successor if Lilu is sold or merged, under this policy.

5. Keeping and deleting information

  • On your device: uninstall Lilu or clear its storage in Android settings. Files Lilu saved to your folders (edits, trimmed copies, ringtones, exports) stay until you delete them.
  • Google Drive backup: delete it in Google Drive as described above.
  • Feedback, purchase records and crash reports we hold: email mcnolgie@gmail.com (include the report number if you sent feedback with Send to Lilu) and we'll delete what we can identify, unless we must keep a purchase record by law or to prevent fraud.

6. Your choices and rights

Most Lilu data never leaves your phone, so you control it directly. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information we hold, or to object to how we use it. Contact us at mcnolgie@gmail.com and we'll respond within a reasonable time.

7. Children

Lilu is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

8. Security

Your library stays in Lilu's private app storage. Listen Together connections are encrypted, and connections to Google services and our server use HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we'll update it here and change the effective date. If a change is significant, we'll also tell you in the app before it takes effect.
