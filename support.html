Support

Need help with Lilu? Email mcnolgie@gmail.com, or use Feedback & Support inside the app. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

How do I add my music?

Open Settings → Manage Folders and add the folders where your music or audiobooks are. Lilu scans them and keeps your library up to date.

Why are some songs missing or dimmed?

A dimmed “Missing” song means the file was moved or deleted outside Lilu. Put the file back, or remove the song from your library. Very short clips can also be hidden by the minimum audio length setting.

How do I get my purchases on a new phone?

Sign in to Google Play with the account you bought with, then open Settings → Store & Purchases → Restore Purchases.

How do I back up my library?

Use Cloud Backup to save a copy to your own Google Drive, or Migration Tools to save a file wherever you choose. Your audio files themselves aren't included, so keep your own copies.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling Lilu removes its data from your phone. Files Lilu saved to your folders (edited tags, trimmed copies, ringtones, exports) stay until you delete them. To remove a Google Drive backup, open drive.google.com → Settings → Manage apps → Lilu → Options → Delete hidden app data. For anything else, email us. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Why does Lilu ask for Bluetooth or the microphone?

Only for features you use: Bluetooth finds friends for Listen Together, and the microphone is for voice search and Listen Together's delay check. Lilu doesn't record or keep audio.
