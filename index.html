Your music and audiobooks, offline. No account, no ads.

Your library, your files

Add your own folders and play MP3, AAC/M4A, M4B, ALAC, FLAC, WAV, AIFF, Ogg and Opus. Edit tags, lyrics and cover art.

Sound your way

10-band equalizer with presets, playback speed, gapless playback, crossfade, sleep timer and an audio trimmer.

Audiobooks done right

Chapters, exact resume, bookmarks and skip controls in a player made for books.

Synced lyrics

Karaoke-style lyrics that follow the song, plus an editor to time your own.

Listen Together

Play the same music in sync with friends nearby, directly between phones.

Everywhere you listen

Widgets, lock-screen and Bluetooth controls, Chromecast, voice search and themes.

Your data stays yours

Lilu works without an account and has no advertising or tracking. Your library lives on your phone. Read exactly what does and doesn't leave your device in the Privacy Policy.
